# Evidence boundaries and limitations

## What was observed
- Recorded responses: 20 accounting cases (110/110 numerical fields), 3 synthetic HR summaries and 3 synthetic market research summaries (30/30 numerical fields). All 26 saved outputs matched reference calculations.
- A prior missing-file scoring attempt incorrectly produced a 0% summary; it was superseded by corrected scoring after the files were located. That missing-file output is not a benchmark failure.
- The original accounting benchmark included historical corrections/identity issues; model, first-attempt, timestamp and intervention provenance remain incomplete. The six-case pilot likewise had 0/6 complete eight-field provenance records at audit time.
- Three **candidate partial-activity** mappings to O*NET 31.0: 13-2011.00/21516, 13-2011.00/21514, 13-1011.00/12874. No full occupational task validated; no independent expert mapping review.
- Primary BLS comparison: 10 detailed occupations × 212 stable-definition metropolitan areas × five years (10,600 complete occupation-metro-year observations).
- Source reconciliation: 19,478 original BLS records matched processed panel keys. Available values agreed; 48 employment entries and 23 mean/median wage entries were missing in both.
- Geographic alternative: occupation-specific paired-endpoint samples of 266–282 metros; the signs of all ten employment and wage changes were unchanged.
- Fixed 2021 metropolitan weights change the real-wage sign for Market Research Analysts and Marketing Specialists from −0.3043% to +0.7290%.

## What has NOT been established
- Independent first-attempt autonomous AI reliability, professional productivity change, or end-to-end professional task performance.
- An externally validated occupational AI-exposure index.
- Effects of AI adoption on employment or pay; no causal identification or linked adoption variable.
- Nationwide representativeness. This is a selected metropolitan complete-case sample, not all U.S. employment.
- Independent third-party replication or a packaged end-to-end public computational environment.

## Interpretation
Claims about saved-output correctness are separate from occupational task mapping, which is separate from descriptive labor-market trends. No inference of automation or displacement follows simply from numerical benchmark agreement. OEWS uses survey-based estimates, changes in methodology and geography, and overlapping panels. CPI-U is national and does not adjust for local costs of living.
