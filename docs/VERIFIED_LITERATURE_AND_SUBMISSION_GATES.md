## Verified source records

1. **Acemoglu, D., & Restrepo, P. (2019).** Automation and New Tasks: How Technology Displaces and Reinstates Labor. *Journal of Economic Perspectives, 33*(2), 3–30. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1257/jep.33.2.3. Publisher: https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jep.33.2.3. **Scope:** conceptual labor-task displacement and reinstatement, not estimated AI impact in this manuscript.
2. **Noy, S., & Zhang, W. (2023).** Experimental evidence on the productivity effects of generative artificial intelligence. *Science, 381*(6654), 187–192. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adh2586. **Scope:** randomized professional writing-task results; not first-attempt reproducibility of the researcher's synthetic accounting/HR/market research records.
3. **Brynjolfsson, E., Li, D., & Raymond, L. R. (2025).** Generative AI at Work. *Quarterly Journal of Economics, 140*(2), 889–942. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjae044. **Scope:** workplace use and productivity evidence; a different design and population from the present study.
4. **Eloundou, T., Manning, S., Mishkin, P., & Rock, D. (2023, revised).** GPTs are GPTs: An Early Look at the Labor Market Impact Potential of Large Language Models. arXiv:2303.10130. DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2303.10130. **Scope:** potential occupational task exposure, not adoption or observed displacement. A later *Science* record may be cited in the existing Word manuscript: use one explicitly verified publication version consistently in the final bibliography.
5. **Gmyrek, P., Berg, J., & Bescond, D. (2023).** Generative AI and Jobs: A Global Analysis of Potential Effects on Job Quantity and Quality. ILO Working Paper 96. DOI: https://doi.org/10.54394/FHEM8239. **Scope:** possible augmentation/automation, not a causal labor estimate here. A separate 2025 refined index is cited in the existing Word manuscript; retain only if its exact publication and citation details are also verified.
6. **U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics.** Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics: Design. https://www.bls.gov/opub/hom/oews/design.htm. **Scope:** OEWS combines six semiannual survey panels over three years; this constrains year-to-year interpretation.
7. **U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics.** OEWS May 2025 technical notes. https://www.bls.gov/oes/CURRENT/oes_tec.htm. **Scope:** model-based MB3 estimation and overlapping source panels.
8. **International Labour Organization.** Publication page for ILO Working Paper 96: https://www.ilo.org/publications/generative-ai-and-jobs-global-analysis-potential-effects-job-quantity-and. Confirms original authors and DOI.
