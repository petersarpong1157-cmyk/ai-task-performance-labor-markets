# Research sources and selected literature

## Original statistical and occupational sources
- U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS), Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS) May metropolitan estimates, 2021–2025: https://www.bls.gov/oes/
- BLS OEWS technical notes and methodology: https://www.bls.gov/oes/oes_doc_arch.htm
- BLS Consumer Price Index, CPI-U annual averages: https://www.bls.gov/cpi/
- O*NET Resource Center, database version 31.0 Task Statements and Task Ratings: https://www.onetcenter.org/database.html

The researcher's local archive audit identified `oesm21all.zip` through `oesm25all.zip` and `db_31_0_excel.zip`. Source files have not been uploaded to this repository.
